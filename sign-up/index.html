Sign up to Property Nudge

Self manage with confidence. £14.99 per property, per month, less for 3 or more, with no long contract.

Just your name, phone number and email to get started. We'll email you a short form to fill in with your property details.

We use your phone number only as a contact detail for your account, not for marketing calls or texts.

What happens next

We'll email you a short form to fill in with your property details, including addresses and certificate dates. You don't need to send your EPC, as we'll look it up on the public register. After that, we'll send you the monthly standing order details. You pay £14.99 per property, per month, dropping to £10.99 for 3 to 5 properties and £9.99 for 6 or more, to the Property Nudge Ltd account, with 30 days' notice, and you can cancel at any time. Full rates are on the pricing page. The account details will be in that later email, not on this page.
