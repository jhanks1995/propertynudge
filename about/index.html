Hi, I'm Jack.

Founder of Property Nudge, based in Ringwood.

Who I am

I've worked in property management and estate agency, and I come from a sales background. The part I've always enjoyed most is the people: getting to know landlords and building relationships that last.

Why I started Property Nudge

In those jobs I kept coming across the same thing. Smoke alarms that had stopped working, gas or electrical certificates that had run out, dates nobody had noticed. Usually it only came to light at a check-in or an inspection, when it was already a problem.

None of those landlords were being careless. They were busy, and the dates are easy to lose track of. So in autumn 2026 I started Property Nudge: a property administration service that emails landlords before each certificate date and keeps them up to date with the rules.

How it works behind the scenes

You send me your properties and dates. I check them back with you, keep them on record and email you in good time before each one is due. When you send the new certificate, I log it and set the next reminder. Once a month you get a newsletter on what's changing.

It's £15 per property per month, and an HMO counts as one property. You pay by monthly standing order and can stop any time with 30 days' notice.

How I work

  • Email only. No app, no logins, nothing to download.
  • Plain English. I'll tell you what's due and what to do, without the jargon.
  • Straight-talking. If I don't know something, I'll say so and point you to GOV.UK.
  • Clear about what I'm not. Property Nudge isn't a letting agent and doesn't give legal advice. You stay in charge of your properties.

If that sounds useful, I'd love to hear from you.
